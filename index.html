About

Mas’s passion lies in theoretical quantum optics and photonics. He is currently engaged in the development of hybrid CV-DV algorithms and quantum control with a focus on trapped-ion platforms. His prior experience encompasses cavity QED and quantum dots for quantum photonic applications, MEMS/NEMS for strain characterization in 2D TMDC materials, and investigation of Single-Electron transistors, gained during his two master’s degrees in Electrical Engineering.
