About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at NC State, where I design hardware-native instruction sets for hybrid qudit–oscillator quantum computation on trapped-ion hardware. Most compilers assume a register of qubits, while a trapped-ion processor natively offers multi-level electronic states and the motional modes of the ion crystal; I build instruction sets that expose that richer hardware to algorithms instead of hiding it behind a qubit abstraction. I also work on quantum signal processing — its robustness to control error and its photonic realization — and on pulse-level control and compilation, mapping logical operations onto the laser and RF drives an ion trap actually executes. Earlier, across two master’s degrees, I studied MEMS/NEMS strain characterization of 2D TMDC materials and single-electron transistors.
